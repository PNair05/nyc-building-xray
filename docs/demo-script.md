# Two-minute demo

1. **Problem (15 seconds)**  
   “An apartment tour tells you very little about a building’s maintenance history. The evidence exists, but it is split across agencies and written for specialists.”

2. **Open the fixture (15 seconds)**  
   Click **Try a demo building**, then **128 Example Avenue**. Point out the persistent **Demo data — fictional example** banner and source coverage row.

3. **Inspect the building view and report (25 seconds)**  
   Rotate the illustrative building view and open one documented floor marker. Then show 11 building-matched 311 reports, two open HPD violations, and the separate DOB complaint total. Emphasize that records without documented floor data are not placed on the model.

4. **Show recurrence (25 seconds)**  
   In **Monthly pattern**, filter to **Heat and hot water**. Note that the current incomplete month is excluded and recurrence means reports in at least three distinct months—it is a transparent product heuristic, not a regulatory conclusion.

5. **Open evidence (20 seconds)**  
   In **Worth investigating**, click the supporting-record link. Show the source category, raw status, date, and official dataset link.

6. **Generate questions (15 seconds)**  
   Click **Explain this building**. With no API key, point out the honest `Summary` label. Read one question about work completed after recurring heat reports.

7. **Close (5 seconds)**  
   “Building X-Ray turns scattered public records into specific questions you can ask before signing.”
